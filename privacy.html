Garricks Ait - Boards: Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Garricks Ait - Boards is a free river-safety app for the Garrick's Ait community on the River Thames. It is published by Peter Stone.

What we collect

If you turn on alerts, your iPhone gives the app a push-notification token. We store that token so we can send you river and weather warnings. It doesn't identify you, and we don't collect your name, email, location, contacts or any other personal information.

What we don't do

No accounts, no tracking, no advertising, no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties. The app doesn't use your location: it covers Garrick's Ait and the Sunbury to Molesey reach only.

Where the data comes from

River data comes from the Environment Agency (Open Government Licence). Weather comes from Apple Weather and Open-Meteo. The app asks those services for river and weather data only; nothing about you is sent to them.

Deleting your data

Turn off notifications for the app in Settings, or delete the app. Your token stops working and is removed automatically.
