Garricks Ait - Boards: Support

Garricks Ait - Boards gives live, early warning of Thames red and amber boards for Garrick's Ait, Hampton (Sunbury Lock to Molesey Lock), using Environment Agency river data that is checked every 15 minutes.

Not getting alerts?

Check Settings › Notifications › Garricks Ait - Boards is on, and that a Focus mode isn't holding them back. In the app, tap "Show more detail", scroll to the bottom, and use "Send a test alert".

Important

The app gives an estimate based on river flow. The official Environment Agency board always takes priority: River Thames current conditions. In an emergency call 999. Floodline: 0345 988 1188.
